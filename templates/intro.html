Vanishing Ink

This is a journal designed to forget you.
But not all at once.

Begin typing, and your words will slowly degrade.
Wait long enough, and it will erode, just as memories do

When you're ready, submit it. But know this:
What you submit is not what you wrote. It's what remains.

The Archive stores every submission.
There are no names, no edits, no deletes.
Only fragments of things people once needed to say.

This is a space for private expression. Write whatever you feel, and watch it fade. There is no saving, only submitting.
